# Northwind Cloud — Company Overview

Northwind Cloud is a fictional B2B software company used for this demo. All documents in this
corpus are synthetic.

## Products

Northwind sells three products: Beacon (observability), Relay (event streaming) and Vault
(secrets management). Every product is sold as a managed service in the EU and US regions.

## Support hours

Standard support is available Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00 GMT. Premium customers have 24x7
coverage with a 15-minute response target for Sev-1 issues.
