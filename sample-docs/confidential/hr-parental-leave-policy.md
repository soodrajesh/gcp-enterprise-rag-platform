# Parental Leave Policy

## Entitlement

Primary carers receive 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary carers receive 8 weeks of fully
paid leave. Leave may be taken flexibly within the first 12 months after birth or adoption.

## How to apply

Submit your request at least 12 weeks before the expected start date to the People team at
people-ops@northwind.example. For questions call Maria O'Brien on 087 555 0199. Employees on
payroll can confirm remaining entitlements in the HR system.
